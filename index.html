Add your signature to Gmail

Takes about a minute.

  1. 01Copy your signature

  2. 02Open Gmail settings

    Open Gmail settings →

    Or: gear icon > See all settings > General tab

  3. 03Create a new signature

    Scroll to Signature, click Create new, name it Swift, and paste into the editor box.

  4. 04Set it as your default

    Under Signature defaults, choose it for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.

  5. 05Put it above quoted replies

    Insert signature before quoted text in replies and remove the "--" line that precedes it.

  6. 06Save

    Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
